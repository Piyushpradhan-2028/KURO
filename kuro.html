      KURO means black. Not the absence of color — the one that holds every other color inside it, unstated.

      This is a personal journal built for one reader: you. No feeds, no likes, no algorithm deciding what matters today. Just a page, a date, and an honest accounting of what happened.

      Five questions, answered daily, are the whole design: What did I do today? What do I need to do? What should I avoid? What are my long-term goals? What did I write before? Everything else is here to support those five.

      Everything you write stays on this device, in your browser's local storage. There is no server, no account, no sync — which means it's yours alone, and also means clearing your browser data will clear your journal. Export or back up anything you can't afford to lose.
